# AI Prompt Engineering Portfolio

**Practical AI Prompts for Business & Data Analysis**

## Overview
A curated collection of practical AI prompts designed for real-world business scenarios. This portfolio demonstrates prompt engineering skills developed through hands-on experience with Claude, ChatGPT, Google NotebookLM, and other AI assistants.

## Contents
1. **Business Analysis Prompts** - Fleet performance, inventory optimization
2. **Data Analysis Prompts** - Excel formulas, data cleaning, dashboard design
3. **Data Labeling Prompts** - ESCI classification, quality review, annotation guidelines
4. **Content Creation Prompts** - Business reports, email templates
5. **Multilingual Prompts** - English, French, Arabic applications
6. **Best Practices** - Prompt engineering techniques and methodologies

## Skills Demonstrated
- Prompt Design & Optimization
- Few-shot & Chain-of-Thought Prompting
- Multilingual Prompting (EN/FR/AR)
- Business Process Automation
- AI-Assisted Data Analysis

## About
This portfolio showcases practical AI prompt engineering for business operations, data analysis, and content creation across three languages.
